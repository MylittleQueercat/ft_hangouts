Introduction

For a successful evaluation, please:

- Remain kind, polite, and respectful throughout the defense.
- Accurately highlight any potential issues in the student's project.
- Keep in mind that different interpretations of the requirements are possible.
Be open-minded and fair when evaluating someone else's vision.

Good luck to all!
Guidelines

REMEMBER: YOU MUST ONLY GRADE WHAT IS PRESENT IN THE STUDENT'S GIT PROJECT.

You must clone the repository and evaluate only its contents.

If the evaluator is not familiar with the project, they must read the entire
subject before starting.
